English

Woof Battle ("the app") is a card game for young children. It collects no personal information at all.

  • It does not ask for or collect names, e-mail addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other personal data.
  • It has no internet access (it does not request the Android INTERNET permission), so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data.
  • It has no in-app purchases, accounts, log-in, chat or links to other apps or websites.

Stored on the device only: the app's settings (players, game length, sound, sound style, auto-play, language, last dog chosen) and the game in progress, in the app's private storage. This never leaves the device, is excluded from cloud backup and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children: the app is made for children aged 3–6 playing with a parent. Because it collects no personal information, it follows the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws such as COPPA and the GDPR. Parent settings are behind a 3-second hold.

Changes: any new version of this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
