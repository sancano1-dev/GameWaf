# Woof Battle — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Woof Battle ("the app") is a card game for young children. It is designed to collect **no
personal information at all**.

## What the app collects
**Nothing.** The app:

- does not ask for or collect names, e-mail addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other
  personal data;
- has **no internet access** (it does not request the Android INTERNET permission), so it cannot
  send anything anywhere;
- contains **no advertising**, **no analytics**, **no tracking** and **no third-party SDKs** that
  collect data;
- has **no in-app purchases**, accounts or log-in;
- has no chat or any way for children to communicate with others.

## What is stored on the device
The app saves only its own settings (number of players, game length, sound on/off, sound style,
auto-play, language and the last dog chosen) and the game in progress, in the app's private storage
on the device. This information never leaves the device, is not shared with anyone, is excluded
from cloud backup, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

## Children
The app is intended for children aged 3–6 playing with a parent's supervision. Because it collects
no personal information, it complies with the Google Play Families Policy and with children's
privacy laws such as COPPA and the GDPR. The parent area is behind a 3-second hold.
