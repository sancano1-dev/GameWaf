## Full description (max 4000)
Woof Battle is the classic "battle" card game made for little ones who can't read or count yet: the bigger dog wins!

🐶 HOW TO PLAY
Everyone taps their paw and flips a card at the same time. The card with the biggest dog wins all the cards. When dogs are the same size it's a WOOF BATTLE: the dogs shout "WOOF!" at each other and the battle goes on until one dog wins. The player who collects all the cards — or the most cards in a short game — is the champion!

👧 MADE FOR TODDLERS (3–6)
• No reading needed: pictures, colours, animations and sounds explain everything
• Huge buttons, simple taps only
• After every round the cards stay on the table until you tap, so children see who won
• Every game starts with a shuffle, so every game is different

🎮 WAYS TO PLAY
• 1 vs 1, 1 vs 2 or 1 vs 3 against friendly computer dogs
• 2, 3 or 4 children on one phone or tablet, each with their own coloured corner
• Demo mode to show how the game works

🐕 CHOOSE YOUR DOG
8 manga-style dogs, each in its own colour: Golden, Dalmatian, Beagle, Pug, Corgi, Poodle, Husky and Dachshund. Each player picks a different one.

✨ ANIME STYLE
Bold ink lines, big shiny eyes, speed lines, power auras, a bark-off on every tie and a happy jumping dog for the winner.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS
• 100% offline: no internet access, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
• Parent area behind a 3-second hold: game length, sound, three sound styles, auto-play, language
• English, Français, Español, Nederlands
• Works on phones and tablets, portrait and landscape
